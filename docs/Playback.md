# Playback

## Play and pause

- Click the video once to play or pause.
- Press **Space**, **Return** or **Enter** on the keypad.
- Choose **Playback ▸ Play/Pause**.
- Press the play/pause media key on your keyboard.

## The timeline

The bar at the bottom shows the elapsed time, the scrubber and the total time.

- Drag the scrubber to move through the video. The picture follows while you drag, and Player goes to the exact point
  when you let go.
- Press **←** or **→** to go back or forward 5 seconds.

A file with no known length has no scrubber.

## Chapters

If a video has chapters, choose **Playback ▸ Previous Chapter** (**⌘←**) or **Playback ▸ Next Chapter** (**⌘→**). **F5**
and **F6** do the same. Without chapters, these items are off.

## Full screen

Double-click the video to enter or leave full screen. Press **Esc** to leave it. In full screen, the pointer hides
along with the controls, and the controls show when you move the pointer over them.

## Pick up where you stopped

Player remembers where you stopped in each video. Open it again, from the welcome screen, **File ▸ Open…** or Finder,
and it continues from there.

Your audio and subtitle choices carry over too. See [Audio and Subtitles](Audio-and-Subtitles.md).

## Play a video that's still downloading

You can open a video while it's still downloading, for example in
[Get](https://github.com/v57/Get/blob/main/docs/README.md). When playback reaches the part that hasn't arrived yet,
Player shows **Waiting for the download** and continues as the file grows.

When Get finishes and moves the file into your Downloads folder, the video stays in Player's recent list, with your
place kept.

## Media keys and Now Playing

The play/pause, next and previous media keys work while Player is open. Next and previous go to the next or previous
video in the same folder. Control Center's **Now Playing** shows the video's name, length and position.
