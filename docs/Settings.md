# Settings

<!-- platforms: macos -->

Choose **Player ▸ Settings…** (**⌘,**). Settings has one pane, **Privacy**.

## Privacy

**Use iCloud** turns iCloud sync on or off. Player uses iCloud for one thing: the list of your devices that
**Open from Device…** offers (see [Open from Device](Open-from-Device.md)). The vault's records are encrypted on
this Mac before they're synced.

It's one switch for every v57 app on this Mac: turning it off here turns it off in the others too. Turning it off asks
first, with **Turn Off iCloud?**. Every v57 app on this Mac then stops syncing through iCloud and iCloud Keychain.
What's already in iCloud stays, and your other devices keep it. Turn the switch back on to sync again.

## Choices kept in the menus

Player's other choices aren't in Settings. They're in the menu bar, and Player remembers them:

- the audio mode and **Even Out Loudness**, in the **Audio** menu ([Audio and Subtitles](Audio-and-Subtitles.md));
- the subtitle style, in **Subtitles ▸ Edit Style…**;
- **Check for Updates Automatically**, in the **Player** menu. It's off when you install Player and checks once a
  day when on.

## On Linux

The Linux build has no Settings window, and iCloud isn't available there.
