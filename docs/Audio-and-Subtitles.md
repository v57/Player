# Audio and Subtitles

## Choose an audio track

The **Audio** menu lists the video's audio tracks, with a check next to the one playing. Choose another to switch; the
picture keeps playing. A track whose format Player can't play is shown but can't be chosen. A video without sound
shows **No audio tracks**.

Surround sound, such as 5.1 Dolby Digital, DTS or AAC, is mixed down to your Mac's speakers or headphones.

## Audio modes

Choose a mode in the **Audio** menu, under **Mode**:

- **Original:** the sound as it is in the file.
- **Balanced:** brings quiet and loud parts a little closer, with a slight lift for the centre channel, where speech
  usually is.
- **Enhance Dialogue:** lifts speech and the centre channel, and makes wide sound effects quieter.
- **Reduce Loud Sounds:** a night mode. Loud effects come down, quiet speech comes up, and there's less bass, so you
  can keep the volume low.

Player remembers the mode for every video. No mode makes the sound clip.

## Even out loudness

Turn on **Audio ▸ Even Out Loudness** to play every video at the same loudness, so you don't have to change the volume
between files. Player measures the loudness as the video plays. It's off until you turn it on, and Player remembers
your choice.

## Subtitles

Subtitles are off until you pick a track. The **Subtitles** menu lists:

- **Off**;
- each subtitle track in the video. SRT, ASS/SSA and WebVTT subtitles show. Image subtitles, such as PGS, are listed but
  can't be chosen. A video without subtitles shows **No subtitle tracks**.

The subtitles show as soon as you pick a track. ASS and SSA subtitles keep their own styling.

## Your choices carry over

When you pick an audio or subtitle track, Player remembers its language and name. The next video you open starts with
the matching track, if it has one.

## Change how subtitles look

1. While a video is open, choose **Subtitles ▸ Edit Style…**. The **Subtitle Style** panel opens at the top right,
   over the video, which keeps playing.
2. Set the **Size**, from 14 to 48 pt.
3. Set the **Width**: **Narrow**, **Medium** or **Wide**. It sets where long lines wrap.
4. Set the **Alignment**: **Left**, **Center** or **Right**.
5. Click **Apply** to keep the style.

Changes show on the video as you make them. When no subtitle is on screen, the panel shows a sample line.
