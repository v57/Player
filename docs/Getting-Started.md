# Getting Started

## The welcome screen

With no video open, Player shows its welcome screen:

- **Open…**: an MKV, MP4 or MOV file on this Mac.
- **Open from Device…**: a video on another of your devices. See [Open from Device](Open-from-Device.md).
- Your recent videos, up to 20, with each one's length, folder and when you last played it. Only videos still on disk
  are listed. Click one to play it from where you stopped.

Before you've played anything, the list says **No Recent Videos**.

## Open a video

Any of these opens a video:

- Choose **File ▸ Open…** (**⌘O**) and pick a file. The panel offers only MKV, MP4, M4V and MOV files.
- Drag a video onto Player's window. It shows **Drop to Play**.
- In Finder, Control-click a video and choose **Open With ▸ Player**, or drop it onto Player's icon in the Dock. This
  works when Player isn't running, too.

If the file isn't a supported type, Player says it's not a supported video file. If Player can't play it, for example
because its codec isn't supported or the file is damaged, it shows **Couldn’t Play the Video** with the reason.

## The window

The video fills the window, under a transparent title bar. The title bar shows the file's name, its video codec and
its length.

While a video plays, the title bar and the controls hide after a few seconds without input. Move the mouse or press a
key to bring them back. They stay up while the video is paused.

Player has one window. Closing it quits Player. Player is always dark, whatever your Mac's appearance.

Player has no volume control or playback speed of its own. Use your Mac's volume.

## Settings and updates

**Player ▸ Settings…** (**⌘,**) has one pane, **Privacy**, with the **Use iCloud** switch. See
[Privacy and Security](Privacy-and-Security.md).

To update Player, choose **Player ▸ Check for Updates…**. Player checks on its own only if you turn on
**Player ▸ Check for Updates Automatically**, which is off when you install it.

**Player ▸ About Player** shows Player's license and the version of FFmpeg it includes, with links to their license
texts.
